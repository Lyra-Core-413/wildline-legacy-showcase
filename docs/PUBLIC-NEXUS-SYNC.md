# Public Nexus presentation alignment

The Reading Room and MorrowWeave website use a consistent Nexus presentation. Their datasets and purposes remain distinct.

| Experience | Content | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Living Legacy Reading Room](https://lyra-core-413.github.io/wildline-legacy-showcase/) | 21 approved curated records; 11 labeled relationships across seven chapters | Explore selected source-grounded project history, its corrections, interpretation and unknowns |
| [MorrowWeave Nexus concept journey](https://morrowweave.com/#galaxy) | 120 fictional nodes; 175 illustrative connections across eight phases | Illustrate a possible connected-life experience and its visual direction |

The [MorrowWeave company site](https://morrowweave.com/) introduces the venture; [Project Wildline on Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/wildline) presents the hackathon project. The public [Reading Room source](https://github.com/Lyra-Core-413/wildline-legacy-showcase) implements the curated experience.

## Shared visual format

The central LYRA context, rotating chapter constellations, nested record orbits and collapsible panels share a visual language. In the Reading Room, selection, search, source inspection and Follow the Footsteps continue to operate on the approved curated records.

Orbital grouping, chapter spokes and decorative dust support navigation. They are not additional source records or historical relationships. The 11 labeled record relationships retain their original meanings: two documented progression references, one documented correction reference and eight thematic reading connections. A line does not establish causality.

## Separate private implementation

The [private Galaxy progress note](PRIVATE-NEXUS-PROGRESS.md) describes the owner-only prototype after its October 18 audit. Its open clusters, local tools, permitted polling and owner access are separate capabilities, not a data connection or feature upgrade for these public maps. The private audit does not certify the truth of every source claim.

## Preserved boundaries

The approved `legacy-data.json` remains the Reading Room's source. The website's fictional data is not merged into it. No private Nexus, Bee or Core endpoint is connected; no generation, embedding, private source import or background synchronization is enabled by this presentation alignment.

The LYRA launcher continues to open ChatGPT separately. It does not transfer selected records or map context, and Voice starts manually.

The current October 18 Devpost Nexus Sync ZIP remains an archived snapshot of commit `cfc65f7c2ae67339bd4231ff66aa365b7ae3ec20`. The current hosted presentation and that archive should be evaluated at their respective revisions. This documentation update does not replace the archive or submit, withdraw or resubmit the Devpost entry. Later default-branch notes are not part of the attached snapshot.

The Reader and Bee Bridge retain their own licenses and test results. This document grants no new license for the Galaxy presentation or artwork and does not combine component test counts into a product-wide result.
## Reproduce the presentation checks

With Node.js 22 or later, run `node --test tests/*.test.mjs` from the repository root. The 18 offline checks cover the immutable approved dataset, record and relationship identity, source links, the ChatGPT launcher boundary, orbital geometry, meaningful zoom, and the actual canvas renderer's parent context. They do not call AI services, Bee or Core.
