# MorrowWeave Living Legacy — reviewer guide

## Start in the Galaxy Reading Room

Open **https://lyra-core-413.github.io/wildline-legacy-showcase/**. The Reading Room is this interactive Galaxy map. It presents a curated chapter of the journey from Bee capture and daily information overload to continuity and the Living Legacy vision.

1. Start with **The whole journey** for all 21 records and 11 labeled relationships.
2. Select a chapter or use search to locate a footstep.
3. Select a star and inspect its narrative and source.
4. Choose **Follow the Footsteps** for a guided reading sequence.
5. Compare source accounts, interpretation, correction and unknowns. A visual relationship is not automatically causal.

The dataset is approved, curated presentation material. It is separate from the private Nexus and makes no live Bee, Core, Graphiti or model call.

The central LYRA context, chapter constellations and nested record orbits follow the Nexus presentation on the [MorrowWeave website](https://morrowweave.com/). Open or close the chapter and story panels to give the map more room. These orbital groups are navigation, not new evidence links. Of the 11 labeled record relationships, three are documented references and eight are thematic reading connections; source inspection retains that distinction.

For the separate public product illustration, open the [Nexus concept journey](https://morrowweave.com/#galaxy): 120 fictional nodes, 175 illustrative connections and eight phases. It is not this curated 21-record chapter, private source history or a larger set of verified historical relationships. Matching the presentation does not connect the datasets or enable background synchronization. See [Public Nexus presentation boundaries](PUBLIC-NEXUS-SYNC.md).

## Legacy model and future work

Living Legacy is about personally selected experiences, relationships, decisions and changing understanding. The current Galaxy demonstrates one curated chapter. Family sharing, broader public synchronization and conversation grounded in selected map context remain future capabilities for this public chapter. Permitted polling and owner-only phone access work separately in the private prototype; they are not supplied by this judge package. The LYRA button opens ChatGPT separately; Voice is manual and receives no automatic map context.

Authorship, correction, privacy and unanswered questions remain part of the design. The AI should not impersonate the person or invent undocumented knowledge. Later family reflections must retain separate authorship.
